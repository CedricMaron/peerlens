# REVIEW — State of the Art

Assess whether the treatment of existing work is adequate to support the gap and
the contribution claims that depend on it.

## Criteria to assess

1. **Relevant approaches covered** — are the main families of approach to this
   problem represented, or only the one the researcher builds on?
2. **Closest prior work identified** — is there an explicit statement of what
   the single nearest piece of work is? Without this, the gap cannot be assessed
   and the contribution cannot be defended.
3. **Recent work** — does the coverage extend to current work, or stop several
   years back? Judge only from dates present in the material.
4. **Contradictory evidence** — is work that disagrees with the researcher's
   position acknowledged and engaged with, or absent?
5. **Important baselines** — are the standard comparisons for this problem
   identified, including ones the researcher has not run?
6. **Accuracy of literature statements** — are characterisations of prior work
   precise and attributable, or sweeping ("existing methods cannot handle X")?
   Sweeping negative claims about entire literatures are a common and serious
   defect.
7. **Verifiability** — are references actual supplied sources, or informal
   mentions that no one has checked?

## Section-specific failure modes to look for

- Prior work described only through the lens of its weaknesses, in a way that
  conveniently justifies the researcher's approach.
- A whole class of methods dismissed in one sentence without evidence.
- The closest competitor missing or discussed only in passing.
- Claims about what prior work "cannot" do, when the material only shows it was
  not evaluated in that setting.
- Coverage assembled from abstracts rather than results.

## Mandatory caution

Never state or imply that no prior work exists, and never treat the supplied
literature list as complete. If coverage looks thin, the correct formulation is
that no equivalent work was identified in the material currently supplied, and
that a targeted search is required. Record that as `missing_information`, not as
a finding of novelty.
